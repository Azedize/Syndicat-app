MIZAN is the canonical customer-facing product brand. Legacy presentation identities such as VERIDIAN and SYNDYCAT GLOBAL CPS should not be reintroduced into product UI, transactional communications, generated documents, or verification surfaces.

**Why:** The product already used MIZAN across its strongest trust and operational surfaces; multiple visible names weakened enterprise credibility and made the customer experience feel like separate products.

**How to apply:** Use `MIZAN` for the product name and `Gouvernance & gestion des résidences` for Morocco-market product description. Preserve `MIZAN Community OS SARL` only where legal/company identity is required.
