You are a Senior Enterprise SaaS Designer, ERP Architect, Government Document Specialist, Product Owner, Business Analyst, and UX Expert.

The document generation system is technically functional, but the overall user experience and visual quality are still far below the level expected from a large SaaS platform. The current PDFs look like basic generated reports rather than official documents produced by a professional condominium management platform. My objective is not only to fix colors or spacing. My objective is to redesign the complete document experience so that every generated document looks trustworthy, premium, official, and enterprise-grade.

Today, most templates share almost the same visual structure. The colors change slightly, but the layouts remain identical. When a user opens a financial report, an election report, a legal document, a meeting report, or a certificate, the experience feels the same. This is not acceptable. Each family of documents must have its own visual identity, hierarchy, typography, spacing, and structure according to its business purpose. Financial documents should look like accounting reports produced by an ERP. Legal documents should look like official administrative documents. Election documents should look like governance and voting reports. Certificates should look like official attestations.

Another major issue is that the syndicate identity is almost invisible. The syndicate logo is not properly highlighted. The building identity is weak. The generated document does not create a strong impression that it belongs to a specific syndicate. Every document must prominently display the syndicate logo, syndicate name, residence identity, and official branding. The visual identity of the syndicate should be visible throughout the document and not only in a small header area.

The signature workflow also appears incomplete. Signed documents do not visibly display the real signatures of the signatories. The PDF must clearly display the actual signature image, the name of the signer, their role inside the syndicate, and the signing date. A signed document should immediately allow a reader to identify who approved it. If signatures exist in the database but do not appear in the PDF, investigate the entire pipeline from database storage to rendering and fix every missing link.

The official stamp also lacks credibility. The current stamp does not resemble a real administrative validation stamp. I want a professional official seal containing the syndicate name, validation status, approval information, and visual elements that make the document appear authentic and legally valid. The stamp must reinforce trust and professionalism.

The document header continues to consume too much vertical space. Even after previous improvements, it still occupies space that should be reserved for useful content. The first page should prioritize document information rather than decorative elements. Reduce wasted space and ensure that the actual content starts much earlier while keeping the branding visible and elegant.

The financial templates require a complete business audit. Many financial documents still request information manually even though the same information already exists inside the database. Analyze every financial template and automatically detect which information can be loaded from budgets, invoices, payment records, charge calls, accounting entries, funds, buildings, lots, members, and syndicates. Users should never be required to manually re-enter information that already exists in PostgreSQL. Only information that truly does not exist in the database should be requested from the user.

I want every template to become entity-driven. A meeting report should be generated from a meeting. An election report should be generated from an election. A financial report should be generated from accounting data. A property certificate should be generated from lot and owner data. A lease contract should be generated from tenant and property data. The user should select the business entity, and the system should automatically load all related information from the database.

I also want a complete audit of all templates to detect missing database relationships. Identify every template that still relies on manual fields and determine which database entities should be linked to it. Create or use relationships such as meetingId, electionId, lotId, memberId, tenantId, invoiceId, budgetId, paymentId, sinistreId, travauxId, and any other entity necessary to generate a complete document automatically.

The final objective is that a generated document should feel comparable to documents produced by banks, public administrations, large ERP systems, and premium SaaS platforms. A user opening the PDF should immediately perceive professionalism, authority, trust, and visual quality. Do not only change colors. Audit the entire document generation ecosystem, redesign the visual system, improve data integration, strengthen branding, display real signatures, optimize layouts, and transform every template into a production-ready enterprise document.
