Real-Life SaaS Subscription Scenario
Step 1 — Youssef Discovers the Platform

Youssef is the newly elected Syndicate Administrator of Résidence Al Amal, a residential complex located in Tangier, Morocco. The residence includes:

5 buildings
120 apartments
95 property owners
40 tenants
12 commercial shops
1 underground parking area

The management committee has decided to replace paper-based management with a modern digital platform.

Youssef downloads the application from the App Store or Google Play and opens it for the first time.

Instead of immediately displaying a Login page, the application presents a professional Welcome Experience introducing the platform's capabilities. Through interactive screens, Youssef discovers features such as financial management, electronic documents, meetings, electronic voting, maintenance tracking, complaints, the marketplace, notifications, and digital signatures.

At the end of the introduction, the application displays the available subscription plans.

Step 2 — Choosing a Subscription Plan

The subscription plans are not hardcoded. They are dynamically loaded from the database so the SaaS owner can modify pricing, features, and limits without updating the application.

Each plan displays:

Plan name
Monthly or yearly price
Maximum number of apartments
Maximum number of users
Storage capacity
Available modules
Support level
Included premium features

For example:

Starter
Up to 50 apartments
5 management users
2 GB storage
Email support
Basic document management
Monthly price
Professional
Up to 300 apartments
Unlimited management users
Electronic signatures
Marketplace
Financial reports
50 GB storage
Priority support
Enterprise
Unlimited apartments
Unlimited users
Advanced governance
API access
Dedicated support
Advanced analytics
Unlimited storage

After comparing the plans, Youssef chooses the Professional Plan, which best fits his residence.

Step 3 — Creating the Customer Account

After selecting a subscription plan, Youssef creates his account by entering:

Full name
Email address
Mobile phone number
Password

The system creates his SaaS customer account, but no residence exists yet.

At this stage, Youssef is simply a new customer of the platform.

Step 4 — Creating the Syndicate

Once the account is created, the platform launches the Initial Configuration Wizard.

Youssef enters the information for his residence:

Syndicate name
Residence name
Logo
Address
City
Postal code
Official email
Phone number
Bank account
IBAN
Tax information (if applicable)

The platform then asks him to configure the physical structure of the residence.

He creates:

5 buildings
120 apartments
12 commercial shops
Underground parking
Common areas

The residence structure is now complete.

Step 5 — Subscription Payment

Before activating the platform, payment is required.

Youssef chooses one of the available payment methods:

Credit or Debit Card
Stripe
PayPal
Bank Transfer
Local Moroccan payment gateway (CMI or equivalent)

After successful payment, the platform automatically:

Creates the subscription
Generates the first invoice
Records the payment
Sets the subscription start date
Sets the subscription expiration date
Marks the subscription as Active

No manual intervention is required.

Step 6 — Automatic Platform Provisioning

Immediately after payment, the SaaS platform automatically provisions a dedicated environment for the customer.

The system:

Creates the new syndicate
Creates an isolated tenant (multi-tenant architecture)
Associates Youssef with this syndicate
Assigns him the Syndicate Administrator role
Applies the limits of the selected subscription plan

From this point onward, Youssef can only access the data belonging to Résidence Al Amal.

He cannot see any information belonging to other customers.

Step 7 — First Login

After activation, Youssef logs into the application.

Because no other users exist yet, the platform asks him to build the management team.

He creates:

Ali → President
Mohamed → Treasurer
Fatima → Secretary
Youssef Idrissi → Committee Member

Each person automatically receives:

An email invitation
Login URL
Temporary password

Upon first login, every user is required to change their password.

Step 8 — Daily Operations Begin

The residence is now fully operational.

Youssef begins adding:

Property owners
Tenants
Apartments
Parking spaces

He assigns each owner to their property and each tenant to the apartment they occupy.

The Treasurer starts managing payments.

The Secretary prepares meetings and official documents.

The President approves decisions and signs documents electronically.

Owners pay their charges and vote during assemblies.

Tenants submit maintenance requests and complaints.

Every user only sees the menus, screens, and actions related to their own role.

Step 9 — Subscription Renewal

Thirty days before the subscription expires, the platform automatically sends reminders.

Notifications appear:

Inside the mobile application
By email
On the administrator dashboard

The Syndicate Administrator can renew the subscription with a single click.

If the subscription expires without payment:

No customer data is deleted.
The subscription status changes to Suspended.
Administrative actions may be restricted according to the SaaS policy.
The administrator is prompted to renew the subscription.

Once payment is completed, the subscription automatically returns to Active, and all services are restored without any loss of data.

Final Business Objective

This workflow mirrors the onboarding process used by leading SaaS platforms such as Microsoft 365, Salesforce, Notion, Monday.com, and Slack:

Discover the platform.
Compare subscription plans.
Create a customer account.
Configure the organization.
Complete the payment.
Automatically provision an isolated workspace.
Invite team members.
Begin daily operations.
Renew the subscription before expiration.

This provides a professional, scalable, and production-ready onboarding experience suitable for a multi-tenant Syndicate Management SaaS.
