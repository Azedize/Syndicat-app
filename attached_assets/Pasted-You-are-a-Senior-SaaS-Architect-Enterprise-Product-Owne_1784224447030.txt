You are a Senior SaaS Architect, Enterprise Product Owner, ERP Consultant, Business Analyst, UX Expert, PostgreSQL Architect, Government Document Specialist, and Digital Transformation Consultant.

The Document Management Module already exists.

The objective is NOT to create a new document module.

The objective is to audit, redesign, optimize, and transform the existing document ecosystem into a true enterprise-grade document platform comparable to SAP, Oracle ERP, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesforce, Odoo Enterprise, and modern government administration systems.

Before making any modification, fully understand the business logic, workflows, permissions, database relationships, document lifecycle, and user interactions.

The current system still behaves too much like a form generator. In many templates, users are required to manually enter information that already exists in the database. This creates duplication, inconsistencies, errors, and a poor user experience. A professional SaaS platform should never ask users to re-enter information that is already available in PostgreSQL.

For every document template, perform a complete business analysis. Identify every field requested from the user and determine whether the information already exists in the system. If the information exists in the database, it must be automatically loaded. The user should only enter information that does not already exist anywhere in the application.

The document generation process must become entity-driven. Documents should not be generated from empty forms. They should be generated from business entities.

For example, a meeting report must be generated from a meeting. An election report must be generated from an election. A financial report must be generated from accounting data. A property certificate must be generated from lot and ownership information. A lease contract must be generated from tenant and property information. A payment receipt must be generated from a payment transaction. An invoice must be generated from invoice records. A budget report must be generated from budgets and budget lines.

When a user selects an entity, the system must automatically retrieve all related information from PostgreSQL and prefill the document. The user should immediately see a live preview containing real data loaded from the system.

Audit all existing templates individually. Do not treat all templates as one group. Analyze each template separately and determine its purpose, its business role, its required entities, its required database relationships, and its expected output.

For every template, detect missing relationships. Identify where meetingId, electionId, lotId, memberId, tenantId, invoiceId, paymentId, budgetId, sinistreId, travauxId, buildingId, syndicateId, or any other business identifier should be linked but currently is not.

Analyze the current PDF engine. Verify whether the generated documents truly reflect the business data of the platform. Detect every hardcoded field, placeholder, fake value, duplicated field, manually typed value, or empty section that should instead be connected to PostgreSQL.

The visual design must also be completely re-evaluated. The current documents still look like simple generated reports. They do not resemble premium enterprise documents. They do not create trust. They do not look official. They do not look like documents produced by a major SaaS platform.

Each category of document must have its own visual identity. Financial reports should look like executive financial statements generated by an ERP. Legal documents should resemble official administrative documents. Meeting reports should look like governance reports. Election reports should resemble institutional voting reports. Certificates should look like official attestations. Contracts should resemble professional legal agreements.

The syndicate branding must become central to every document. The syndicate logo must be automatically loaded from the database and displayed prominently. The syndicate name, building identity, registration information, address, and contact details must be integrated into the visual identity of the document. Documents should immediately communicate which syndicate issued them.

The signature workflow must be audited end-to-end. Verify document_signatures, users, office holders, storage, APIs, and PDF rendering. Every signed document must display the actual signature image, the name of the signer, the role of the signer, and the signing date. Placeholders such as “Pending Signature” should only appear when a document is truly unsigned.

The official stamp must also be redesigned. The current appearance does not create confidence. The stamp should resemble a real administrative seal, containing the syndicate identity, approval information, validation status, and visual elements that reinforce authenticity and legal validity.

The QR verification system must be audited. Every QR code must lead to a real verification page. The verification page should display document status, document number, issue date, signatures, issuing syndicate, and verification result.

Review all document workflows. Analyze creation, draft mode, approval, publication, signing, versioning, archiving, cancellation, expiration, and verification. Ensure every state transition is logical, secure, and auditable.

Verify the permissions matrix. Super Admin should manage templates and global governance. Syndicate Admins should manage their own documents. Members should only access documents they are authorized to view. Tenants should only see documents allowed by business rules. Every access must respect multi-tenant isolation.

Audit the template management system itself. Determine whether templates are stored in code, database, HTML, JSON, or another format. Evaluate whether a professional Template Designer should be introduced for Super Admins. The objective is to allow template evolution without requiring source-code modifications for every change.

For every template, generate a detailed report including:

Current Business Purpose

Current Data Sources

Missing Data Sources

Manual Fields That Should Be Automated

Missing Relationships

Design Quality Score

Database Integration Score

Enterprise Readiness Score

Security Risks

User Experience Problems

Recommended Improvements

After completing the audit, implement improvements template by template. Fully complete one template before moving to the next. Do not partially improve multiple templates simultaneously.

The final objective is to create a document management system where users feel they are working inside a world-class SaaS platform. Every generated document should feel official, trustworthy, intelligent, data-driven, visually premium, legally credible, and deeply integrated with the business processes of the condominium management ecosystem.
