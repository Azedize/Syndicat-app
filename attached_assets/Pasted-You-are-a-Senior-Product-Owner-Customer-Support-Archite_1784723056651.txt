You are a Senior Product Owner, Customer Support Architect, SaaS Business Analyst, Help Desk Expert, Enterprise Workflow Designer, and Property Management Specialist.

Your mission is to design and implement a complete support ecosystem based on real-life business scenarios.

The support system must be intuitive, professional, and adapted to the daily reality of syndicates, residents, owners, tenants, administrators, and the platform operator.

==================================================
SCENARIO 1
ALI HAS A PROBLEM WITH HIS MONTHLY PAYMENT
==========================================

Ali owns an apartment in Résidence Al Andalous in Tangier.

Every month, Ali pays his syndicate contribution through the application.

One day, Ali notices that his payment was deducted from his bank account but still appears as "Unpaid" inside the application.

Ali becomes worried because he does not want to receive a reminder or a penalty.

Ali opens the application and clicks:

Support Center → New Ticket

The application automatically fills:

* Ali's name
* Residence
* Building
* Apartment number
* Owner account
* Contact information

Ali selects:

Category:
Payment Problem

Subject:
Payment not reflected

Description:
I paid my contribution three days ago but it still appears unpaid in the application.

Ali uploads:

* Bank receipt
* Screenshot of the transfer

The ticket is created.

Immediately:

The Syndicate Administrator receives a notification.

The platform administrator does NOT receive anything.

This issue belongs to the syndicate.

==================================================
SCENARIO 2
FATIMA REPORTS A WATER LEAK
===========================

Fatima rents an apartment in the same residence.

She discovers water leaking from the ceiling of the underground parking area.

Fatima opens:

Support Center → Maintenance Request

She creates a ticket.

Category:
Maintenance

Priority:
High

Description:
Water is leaking near parking space P-17 and the floor is becoming slippery.

Fatima uploads photos.

The ticket is automatically assigned to:

* Syndicate Administrator
* Maintenance Manager

The ticket never reaches the platform administrator because this is an operational issue inside the residence.

==================================================
SCENARIO 3
AHMED (SYNDICATE ADMIN) DISCOVERS A PLATFORM BUG
================================================

Ahmed is the Syndicate Administrator.

He is preparing official documents for the annual assembly.

Several owners report that electronic signatures are no longer working.

Ahmed tests the feature himself.

The problem affects multiple users.

Ahmed understands that this is not a residence problem.

This is a platform problem.

Ahmed opens:

Administration → Platform Support

Ahmed creates a support request.

Category:
Technical Bug

Priority:
Critical

Subject:
Electronic signatures no longer work

Description:
Several owners are unable to sign documents. The issue affects multiple devices and multiple users.

This ticket is sent directly to:

* Platform Super Admin
* Technical Team
* Application Support Team

Residents cannot access this support channel.

==================================================
SCENARIO 4
NADIA REQUESTS A NEW FEATURE
============================

Nadia manages a large syndicate with several buildings.

She wants to add QR codes on visitor badges.

The feature does not exist.

Nadia opens:

Platform Support → Feature Request

Description:
Our residence needs QR-based visitor badges for security control.

The ticket is classified as:

Feature Request

The platform team receives it.

The request enters the product roadmap process.

==================================================
SCENARIO 5
MOHAMED CANNOT LOGIN
====================

Mohamed is a resident.

He forgot his password.

He tries several times.

The application suggests:

Reset Password

If the reset process works, no support ticket is needed.

If the reset process fails, Mohamed can create a ticket.

The ticket is sent first to the Syndicate Administrator.

The Syndicate Administrator verifies:

* Account status
* Residence assignment
* User role

Only if the issue is technical and cannot be solved locally may the administrator escalate it to Platform Support.

==================================================
ESCALATION RULES
================

A resident can contact only the Syndicate Administration.

A tenant can contact only the Syndicate Administration.

An owner can contact only the Syndicate Administration.

A committee member can contact only the Syndicate Administration.

The platform team must never receive hundreds of resident tickets directly.

The Syndicate Administration acts as the first support level.

The Platform Team acts as the second support level.

==================================================
SUPPORT HIERARCHY
=================

Level 1

Resident
→ Syndicate Administration

Owner
→ Syndicate Administration

Tenant
→ Syndicate Administration

---

Level 2

Syndicate Administration
→ Platform Support

---

Level 3

Platform Support
→ Technical Team

==================================================
REAL BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
=======================

The platform administrator should manage platform issues.

The syndicate administrator should manage residence issues.

Residents should never directly contact the platform operator.

Every ticket must follow a clear business workflow.

Every action must be traceable.

Every ticket must have:

* Status
* Priority
* Owner
* History
* Attachments
* Notifications
* Escalation path

The support system must behave like a professional SaaS product used by thousands of residences and syndicates.
