==================================================
REAL-LIFE SCENARIO 1
AHMED SELLS HIS OLD WASHING MACHINE
===================================

Ahmed is a resident of Résidence Al Andalous.

Ahmed bought a new washing machine.

He wants to sell his old washing machine to another resident.

Ahmed opens:

Marketplace → Sell Product

The system asks Ahmed to provide:

Product Information:

* Product title
* Category
* Brand
* Model
* Condition
* Purchase year
* Description
* Selling reason
* Original price
* Selling price
* Negotiable or not

Location:

* Building
* Block
* Floor

Images:

* Main image
* Additional images
* Product video (optional)

Contact Preferences:

* Internal platform chat
* Phone
* Email

==================================================
PUBLICATION WORKFLOW
====================

After Ahmed submits the listing:

The product MUST NOT become public.

Status:

PENDING REVIEW

The system automatically:

* Scans images
* Scans title
* Scans description
* Checks prohibited words
* Checks spam indicators
* Checks duplicate products

The listing is then sent to Marketplace Moderation.

==================================================
ADMIN REVIEW PROCESS
====================

Marketplace Admin receives a notification.

The Admin reviews:

* Product title
* Product category
* Description
* Images
* Price
* Seller identity

The Admin can:

APPROVE

REJECT

REQUEST MODIFICATION

==================================================
IF APPROVED
===========

Status becomes:

ACTIVE

The product appears inside:

Marketplace → Community Store

Visible to:

* Owners
* Residents
* Tenants
* Authorized users

The listing displays:

* Product images
* Seller name
* Building
* Price
* Date published
* Product condition

The system sends Ahmed:

"Your product has been approved and published."

==================================================
IF REJECTED
===========

Status becomes:

REJECTED

Admin provides reason:

Examples:

* Missing images
* Inappropriate content
* Wrong category
* Prohibited item
* Duplicate listing

Ahmed receives notification.

The product remains hidden.

==================================================
IF MODIFICATION REQUESTED
=========================

Status:

NEEDS CHANGES

Ahmed receives:

* Requested modifications
* Detailed comments

Ahmed edits the listing.

The listing returns to:

PENDING REVIEW

==================================================
REAL-LIFE SCENARIO 2
FATIMA BUYS THE PRODUCT
=======================

Fatima lives in the same residence.

Fatima opens Marketplace.

She finds Ahmed's washing machine.

She can:

* View images
* Zoom images
* Read description
* Compare prices
* Contact Ahmed

She clicks:

Contact Seller

An internal conversation starts.

==================================================
RESERVATION PROCESS
===================

Fatima wants to buy the product.

She clicks:

Reserve Product

Status becomes:

RESERVED

Other users see:

Reserved by another resident.

==================================================
PURCHASE COMPLETION
===================

Ahmed and Fatima meet.

The transaction is completed.

Ahmed clicks:

Mark as Sold

Status becomes:

SOLD

The product is archived.

The listing remains visible as sold history.
