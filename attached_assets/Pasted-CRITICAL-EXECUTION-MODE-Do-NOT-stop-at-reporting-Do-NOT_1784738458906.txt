CRITICAL EXECUTION MODE

Do NOT stop at reporting.

Do NOT only generate audits.

Do NOT only generate recommendations.

You must actively inspect the entire application and continuously improve it until it reaches enterprise production quality.

For every screen:

1. Analyze its business purpose.
2. Analyze who should use it.
3. Analyze who should not see it.
4. Analyze its workflows.
5. Analyze all buttons.
6. Analyze all APIs.
7. Analyze all database dependencies.
8. Analyze all notifications.
9. Analyze all permissions.
10. Analyze all edge cases.

For every issue found:

* Explain the root cause.
* Explain the business impact.
* Implement the fix.
* Verify the fix.
* Re-test the workflow.
