* Real business value
* Frequency of use
* Legal importance
* User demand

* Duplication with other templates

CATEGORY A — Mandatory for V1
Templates required for production launch.

CATEGORY B — V1.5
Useful templates but not required for launch.

Templates with low business value, duplicate purpose, or rarely used.
