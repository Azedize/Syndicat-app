- Incidents
- Documents
- Notifications
- Messaging
- Marketplace
- Audits
- Reports

The objective is to digitize the complete operation of a Moroccan condominium.

==================================================
BUSINESS MODEL
==================================================

Understand the relationships:

Residence
 └── Building
      └── Lots
            ├── Owner
            └── Tenant

Owner:

- owns one or more lots
- pays charges
- votes in AG
- receives documents
- can sell products in marketplace
- can communicate with syndic

Tenant:

- occupies a lot
- receives notifications
- communicates with syndic
- may submit incidents

Syndic:

- manages buildings
- validates payments
- manages charges
- manages AG
- manages incidents
- manages documents

Super Admin:

- manages all syndic organizations
- manages all users
- audits platform
