==================================================
SCENARIO 1
ALI REQUESTS A RESIDENCE CERTIFICATE
====================================

Ali owns Apartment B-204 in Résidence Al Amal in Tangier.

Ali wants to register his child in a new school.

The school asks for proof that he lives at the residence.

Ali opens the mobile application.

He goes to:

Documents
→ Request Document
→ Residence Certificate

Ali should not manually type information already available in the database.

The application automatically retrieves:

* Full name
* CIN
* Residence
* Building
* Apartment number
* Ownership status
* Address

Ali only clicks:

Submit Request

The request enters a workflow.

The secretary receives the request.

The secretary verifies:

* Ali is an active owner
* Ali has a valid lot
* No ownership dispute exists

The secretary validates.

The president receives a notification.

The president signs electronically.

The document becomes available.

Ali receives:

"Your Residence Certificate is ready."

Ali downloads the PDF.

SCENARIO 2
FATIMA REQUESTS A PAYMENT CERTIFICATE
=====================================

Fatima has paid all her charges.

She wants proof of payment to complete a bank file.

She opens:

Documents
→ Payment Certificate

The system automatically retrieves:

* Payments
* Charges
* Balance
* Outstanding debt

The application calculates:

Total paid
Remaining balance
Last payment date

Fatima must not manually enter any amount.

The document is generated automatically.

If the balance is positive:

Certificate approved.

If unpaid charges exist:

Certificate blocked.

The system informs Fatima:

"This certificate cannot be issued because outstanding charges remain."

SCENARIO 6
TENANT VS OWNER
===============

A tenant and an owner do not have the same rights.

Example:

Fatima is a tenant.

She can request:

* Residence Certificate
* Complaint Report
* Maintenance Requests

She cannot request:

* Ownership Certificate
* Governance Reports
* Financial Reports
* Audit Reports

Ali is an owner.

He can access additional documents.

The application must automatically adapt available documents based on user role.

==================================================
SCENARIO 7
SMALL SYNDICATE VS LARGE SYNDICATE
==================================

A residence with 15 apartments does not need the same documents as a residence with 500 apartments.

Small Syndicate:

Uses mostly:

* Residence Certificates
* Payment Certificates
* Meeting Minutes
* Convocations

Large Syndicate:

Uses:

* Financial Reports
* Audit Reports
* Governance Reports
* Recovery Documents
* Legal Files
* Contractor Reports

The SaaS must allow each syndicate to activate only the documents it needs.

==================================================
DOCUMENT DESIGN STRATEGY
========================

All syndicates should NOT have completely different designs.

Instead:

Create document families.

Family 1:
Certificates

Family 2:
Finance

Family 3:
Governance

Family 4:
Legal

Family 5:
Operations

Family 6:
Administration

Each family shares:

* Typography
* Header structure
* Footer structure
* Signature zone
* QR verification area

Only content changes.

This guarantees professional consistency.

Analyze every document.
